Haptic Perception and Human-Machine Interaction

A haptic interaction project built around the Phantom Omni device and a virtual paper-writing environment.

  • Acquired pose data and interaction states in real time.
  • Collected end-effector motion information.
  • Simulated contact with a virtual writing plane.
  • Generated constraint forces for basic haptic feedback.
